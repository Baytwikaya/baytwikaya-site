Privacy Policy

This page explains how the Bayt wikaya centre website collects your data, how it uses it, and what your rights are. We respect your privacy: we do not sell your data and we do not share it with any third party for marketing purposes.

About us

This website is the official website of the Bayt wikaya centre, a cupping therapy, bone alignment and massage centre in Casablanca.

  • Address: Al Fidaa, Al Qariaa, Rue 57 No. 46, behind Pharmacie Al Anbar, Casablanca.
  • Phone: 0664651301
  • Email: contact@baytwikaya.site

What data we collect

Data you give us yourself

When you request an appointment through the booking form on the website, we collect the information you write in it: your full name, phone number, the service requested, and your preferred appointment date. We use this information only to reply to your request and to contact you about your appointment.

When you write to us by email or WhatsApp, we keep what you send us in order to reply to you.

Usage data

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage data about your visit to the website, such as the pages you visit, your device and browser type, and the length of your visit. This data does not identify you personally, and we use it only to understand how the website is used and to improve it.

How we use your data

  • To reply to booking requests and to contact you about your appointment.
  • To improve the website and our services, based on anonymous usage data.
  • To comply with any legal obligation, when needed.

We do not sell your personal data to anyone, and we do not share it with third parties except in the following cases: with the technical service providers who help us run the website (such as Google Analytics and Google AdSense), and only as far as necessary for these services to work, or when the law requires it.

Cookies

This website uses cookies, which are small files stored on your device. Some of them are necessary for the website to work, and some are used to analyse usage (Google Analytics) and to display ads. You can delete cookies or block them from your browser settings, but some website features may not work fully then.

Advertising and Google AdSense

This website may display ads through Google AdSense. Google uses cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this website or to other websites. These include the DoubleClick cookie used by Google and its partners to show ads.

You can turn off personalised ads (ads based on your interests) at any time from the Google ads settings page at adssettings.google.com, or from www.aboutads.info/choices.

Data retention and security

We keep booking data only for as long as needed to reply to your request and follow up on your appointment, and no longer than necessary. We take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorised access, but the security of any data transfer over the internet cannot be absolutely guaranteed.

Your rights

At any time, you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, or to correct or delete it. To make any such request, or for any question about this policy, write to us at contact@baytwikaya.site and we will reply as soon as possible.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, and we will publish the updated version on this page with the last-update date.
