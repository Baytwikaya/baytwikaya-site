Consult a qualified doctor first

Before starting any treatment, before undergoing cupping therapy (hijama), before changing any medicine or adjusting its dose, and before changing your diet or starting a new exercise programme, consult a qualified doctor or qualified health professional. Tell your doctor about your full health condition, including chronic illnesses, the medicines you take, pregnancy, and any previous surgery, so that they can give you advice suited to your case.

If you have symptoms that persist or get worse, or if your condition worsens after reading any information on this website, see a doctor without delay, and do not rely on the content of this website alone when making your decision.

Individual experiences or examples mentioned in articles do not mean that you will obtain the same result, and they must not be understood as a promise of a particular outcome.

Emergencies

In an emergency, call the emergency services immediately or go to the nearest hospital. Symptoms that require immediate help include severe or pressing chest pain, difficulty breathing, loss of consciousness, heavy bleeding that does not stop, and signs of a stroke such as sudden weakness in the face or arm or sudden difficulty speaking. Do not wait, and do not try to treat these conditions yourself or on the basis of information from the internet.

The centre's services do not replace medical care

The services of the Bayt wikaya centre, including cupping therapy, bone alignment and massage, are complementary services. They do not replace medical care, and they do not remove the need to follow up with your doctor or to follow the treatment they prescribe.
