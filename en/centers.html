What does the Smart Assistant do?

Six practical features, designed from inside a real center.

🗺️ Interactive point map

154 points on back, front, face and limb maps — tap to select directly on the map.

🤖 Smart Assistant

Choose up to 3 conditions at once, and it suggests the right points — 72 ready conditions, and add any point manually by its number.

☪️ Sunnah and preventive points

Ready sets in one tap: Sunnah points, preventive points, or both together.

🗂️ Full documentation

Client files with quick search, a session log, a printable session sheet, and informed consent before each session.

🔒 Strong protection

PIN lock, encrypted backups (AES-GCM), and 100% local data.

🔄 Automatic updates

When a new version is released, the app alerts you and updates itself in one tap — and your licence and data move with you automatically.

Frequently asked questions

How do I activate the app?

Download the version from this page, request the licence via WhatsApp, pay by bank transfer, and receive the code for your center's name — enter it on the activation screen.

Does the tool work without internet?

Yes, daily use is fully local on your device: clients, sessions and backups. Internet is only needed for the initial download and for update checks.

Can I use it on more than one device?

One licence is for one device, by name.

How do updates reach me?

The app checks automatically: when a new version is released, an alert appears, and one tap downloads and installs it — and your licence and data move with you automatically.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer — order via WhatsApp (from CIH, activation is instant; from another bank, after the transfer arrives).

What are the system requirements?

A Windows version (computer). An official installer: install it on your computer, and you will find a shortcut on the desktop and in the Start menu; uninstall from the Control Panel.
